    01The project

    Room to breathe, on the Red Sea.

    The masterplan cascades with the natural terrain, so homes step down the slope and every one of them looks out over the Red Sea.

    Low density by design
    The plan sets a limit on how many homes sit on each square metre, so there is more space between them and more privacy in each one.
    Built around the outdoors
    Homes are designed around usable outdoor space rather than token balconies, for a coast where you live outside most of the year.
    Facing the water
    The layout is arranged to give homes views over the Red Sea.
    Shared space you can walk to
    Public areas are positioned to be easy to reach on foot, with room for a range of activities rather than a single clubhouse.
    Inside Somabay
    On the coast of an award-winning destination that is already built and running, rather than on an empty stretch of shoreline.
