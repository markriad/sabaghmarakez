01The project

A neighbourhood where everyone knows your name.

Crescent Walk takes its cue from the garden neighbourhoods of old Cairo — the ones where children roamed freely and neighbours greeted each other by name. 118 acres in East Cairo, organised around a green pathway that runs the length of the community, widening into parks and narrowing into a cycling lane before it reaches your back gate.

i

Connected without the traffic

Direct access to Sokhna Road, South Teseen and the Mid Ring Road. Close enough to reach the city quickly, far enough not to hear it.

ii

Green space came first

Most compounds lay roads and fit gardens into what's left. Here the pathway was drawn first and the homes arranged along it.

iii

Built for the climate

Brickwork, masonry banding and timber hues, with double-height ceilings and deep terraces that handle Cairo's heat.

04Why Crescent Walk

What the green spine actually gives you.

i

A back gate onto the park

Homes open directly onto the green spine — no walking around the block to reach it.

ii

A full cycling loop

The path becomes a dedicated lane lined with flowering shrubs and benches, looping the whole community.

iii

Shops without leaving

The commercial park carries groceries, cafés and daily essentials inside the gates.

iv

Art deco, built for heat

PBD's architecture uses brick, masonry banding and deep terraces — nostalgic, but designed for the climate.
