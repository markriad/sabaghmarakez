01The project

A beach town built to be walked barefoot.

Ramla sits on the calmest stretch of Egypt's north coast, where the bay is sheltered and the sand runs white. At high tide the water reads as an unbroken sheet of turquoise; at low tide it reveals tidal pools carved naturally into the seabed — shallow, warm, and safe enough for children to spend the whole morning in.

i

Everything within walking distance

Soft sand paths link the neighbourhoods to the beach, the town and each other. No car needed once you arrive.

ii

Oriented for the breeze

Every building is positioned to let the Mediterranean wind move through the neighbourhoods rather than around them.

iii

Designed around the land

The masterplan works with the site's existing landscape and water instead of levelling it flat first.

04Why Ramla

What you won't find elsewhere on the coast.

i

Natural tidal pools

Shallow warm pools carved into the seabed, revealed at low tide. Rare on this coastline.

ii

A swimmable lagoon

A lake at the centre of the development for swimming and dining, separate from the sea.

iii

Adrère Amellal lodge

The Siwa eco-lodge is opening a 71-room nature lodge here, built the same earth-construction way.

iv

Year-round sports campus

Football, padel, tennis and a semi-Olympic pool, run by the country's top academies through summer.
