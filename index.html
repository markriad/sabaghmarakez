02About Marakez

The developer behind Mall of Arabia.

Marakez built the first park inside a shopping centre in Egypt, the first residential tower outside downtown Cairo, and the first international-standard mall in a governorate city. Its portfolio spans Mall of Arabia, Mall of Tanta, Mall of Mansoura, Town Center, D5M, Aeon and Mindhaus.

i

Part of Fawaz Al Hokair Group

Founded 1989. Over 1.5 million square metres of retail across 21 shopping malls in 11 countries.

ii

Retail comes built in

Because Marakez develops malls first, its residential projects have shops and workspace in the plan rather than promised later.

iii

International design partners

WATG master-planned both Ramla and Crescent Walk; PBD Architects handled the Crescent Walk architecture.
