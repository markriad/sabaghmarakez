01The project

Five journeys, one destination.

District 5 is Marakez’s flagship East Cairo development — 268 acres on the Cairo–Ain Sokhna highway at the centre of New Katameya. Because Marakez builds malls first, the retail and workspace are part of the plan rather than promised for a later phase: homes, the D5M mall, Mindhaus offices and the Clubfive sports club all share one address.

i

Connected both ways

Two bridges built over the Ain Sokhna highway link District 5 back to New Cairo. Road 90 in seven minutes, the airport in nineteen.

ii

Wadi Degla on the doorstep

The protectorate runs along the southern edge, so open desert is minutes away when you want it.

iii

Everything walkable

Trails, bike lanes and landscaped parks connect the neighbourhoods to the mall, the offices and the club.

Mindhaus

Workspace inside the gates.

Marakez’s office product at District 5, split between a campus and a strip, both in the non-residential zone with their own road access.

Boutique office buildings

Mindhaus Strip

For established medium-sized businesses wanting an independent building rather than a floor in someone else’s. Sellable offices in G+3 and G+4 formats, arranged as Privates, Twins and Quads — one, two or four vertical units, each with its own entrance.

  • 600–2,400 sqm
  • G+3 / G+4
  • Private · Twin · Quad
  • Independent entrances

Campus modules

Mindhaus Campus

106,000 sqm of land with over 60,000 sqm of leasable office and retail across more than 110 modules, arranged around communal piazzas. Each module is built for daylight and can be divided or combined, with the option of adding a mezzanine.

  • 110+ modules
  • 60,000 sqm leasable
  • Divisible layouts
  • Mezzanine option

Unit formats

Privates, Twins & Quads

Each building divides into one, two or four separate vertical units. Every unit gets its own entrance and its own common areas, so a business can take a whole building or a quarter of one without sharing a lobby.

  • Private — whole building
  • Twin — two units
  • Quad — four units
  • Mezzanine option
