    01The project

    Five journeys, one destination.

    Homes, a mall, an office campus and a sports club share a single address — which is unusual, and comes down to the order they were built in.

    Retail came first
    Because the malls are built before the homes, D5M and Mindhaus are part of the plan rather than promised for a later phase.
    Wadi Degla on the doorstep
    The protectorate runs along the southern edge, so open desert is minutes away when you want it.
    Suburban, city conveniences
    A residential community with the shops, workspace and sport usually only found in the middle of the city.

    Workspace inside the gates.
